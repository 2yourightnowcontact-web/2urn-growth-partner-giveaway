# Valor Free Roof Giveaway — Matt's VSL Script

**On camera:** Matt Willard, on a job site or in front of a finished roof, Catch-All cover visible if possible. **Length:** 2:30 to 3:00. **Runs:** top of the giveaway page; sections marked ✂ are the 15/30-second ad cuts.
**Audience:** Middle Tennessee homeowners with a roof over 10 years old. Problem-aware at best. Most have never called a roofer and are afraid of being sold.

**Belief strategy.** The homeowner already wants a sound roof (outcome). What blocks them is fear of the roofer (credibility) and the belief that an inspection is a sales trap (solution). So Matt opens by naming that fear, explains the giveaway as the reason he gets to meet people before the leak, and makes the free inspection the no-risk step. Proof is the real numbers: 5.0 on Google, 56 reviews, BBB A+ since 2020, 3,000 roofs, warranty in writing.

---

## 0:00 — Hook ✂
> I'm Matt with Valor Roofing in Murfreesboro. This December, one family in Middle Tennessee is getting a brand-new roof from us. Free. Installed in a day.
>
> And everybody who enters gets something real, even if they don't win. Give me two minutes and I'll explain how it works and why I'm doing it.

## 0:20 — Name the fear
> Here's the honest truth about my business. Most people only call a roofer when water's already coming through the ceiling.
>
> And by then you're stressed, you're in a hurry, and you're trying to figure out which of the five guys who knocked on your door after the storm you can actually trust.
>
> I don't want to meet you that way.

## 0:45 — Reason why
> We've put on more than three thousand roofs around here. We're A-plus with the Better Business Bureau, have been since 2020. Five stars on Google, fifty-six reviews, none of them edited.
>
> So we're doing something a little different. Instead of waiting for the leak, we're giving a roof away. One drawing, December 11th. One family wins a full Owens Corning replacement, up to thirty squares, at no cost.

## 1:10 — Second prize ✂
> Now, more than one family wins second prize. Second prize is your choice: twenty-five hundred dollars off a full replacement, or a free upgrade to Class 4 impact-resistant shingles, the ones that stand up to hail.
>
> To claim it, all you do is book a free roof inspection with us within seventy-two hours of the drawing. That's it.

## 1:35 — What the inspection is (and isn't)
> Let me tell you what that inspection is, because I know what you're thinking.
>
> We come out, we get on the roof, we photograph every slope, and we hand you a written report. If your roof's fine, we tell you it's fine. Ask Jon Kelley. We came out next day, found a small problem, and fixed it for nothing.
>
> Forty-five minutes. No pressure. You keep the report either way.

## 2:00 — Trust beside the action
> And if you do need a roof, here's what you get with us. Five-year workmanship warranty, in writing. A free bump from twenty-five to thirty-year shingles on every replacement. And our Catch-All system on every tear-off, so the old shingles and nails land on a cover, not on your siding and your flower beds.

## 2:20 — Close ✂
> So here's what to do. Hit the button, put in your name and address, takes thirty seconds. Then share your link with a neighbor. If the neighbor you referred wins, you get five thousand dollars off your own roof.
>
> The drawing's December 11th. The timer's right there on the page. I'll see you on the roof.

---

## Ad cuts
- **15s:** Hook lines 1 and 2 + "Hit the button, thirty seconds, drawing December 11th."
- **30s:** Hook + Second prize + Close.
- **Hook variants:**
  1. "One Middle Tennessee family is getting a free roof from us in December. Here's how you enter."
  2. "I'm a roofer. I'd rather meet you before the leak. So we're giving a roof away."
  3. "Twenty-five hundred off or free Class 4 shingles. Everybody who enters wins one. Here's the catch: there isn't one."

## Belief audit
| Brick | Where |
|---|---|
| Outcome | "Brand-new roof. Free. Installed in a day." |
| Identity | "I don't want to meet you that way"; the homeowner who plans ahead |
| Problem | Calling a roofer only after the leak, picking from door-knockers |
| Solution | Free inspection with photos and a written report, no pressure |
| Product | Owens Corning system, 30-year upgrade, Catch-All, 5-year workmanship |
| Credibility | 3,000 roofs, BBB A+ since 2020, 5.0 / 56 reviews, Jon Kelley story |

## Do not say
Anything about insurance deductibles being covered, waived or absorbed. Any savings number not in Valor's own records. "Everybody wins" (say "more than one family wins second prize").
