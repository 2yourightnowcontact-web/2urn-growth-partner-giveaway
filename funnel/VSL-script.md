# 2URN Growth Partner Giveaway — VSL Script (v2, after the Nov 13 Hydra call)

**On camera:** Camron. **Shoot:** Nov 17. **Edit:** Shabi. **Length:** 3:00 to 3:30. **Where:** top of the giveaway page; ✂ marks the 15/30/60-second ad cuts.
**Tone, per Josh:** fun. This is a giveaway, not a webinar. Goofy is allowed in the open and the close. The qualification block in the middle is dead serious.
**Audience:** owners of roofing, HVAC and window companies doing $1M+, heading into the slow season, burned by at least one agency, tired of the "are you a roofer doing a million a year" hook they see from every agency.

**Belief strategy.** They already believe leads matter. What's broken: solution belief ("agencies don't work, every ad looks the same"), identity belief ("I'm not a marketer, I don't want to be on camera"). The script agrees with the cynicism, reframes the hidden lock (nobody recognizes you, and nobody calls the leads), makes owner-on-camera the master key, and uses the qualification rules as the thing that makes the prize credible. Winning requires being the kind of operator who'd succeed anyway.

---

## 0:00 — Hook ✂ (fun)
> It's winter. Your door-knockers quit. Your referrals dried up. And every agency on earth is in your inbox asking if you're a roofer doing a million a year.
>
> I'm Camron with 2URN Marketing. We're going to run one roofing, HVAC or window company's entire marketing for ninety days. Free. Through the slow season.
>
> And second place wins big too. Stick around.

## 0:23 — Before you click (the rules, said straight)
> Here's who this is for, because I'm picking the winner myself, and I'm picking a case study, not a lottery ticket.
>
> One. You did a million or more in the last twelve months. Did, not "about to."
>
> Two. You, or your lead rep, are on camera in the ads. Real face, real voice. No AI avatars, no stock footage. Homeowners trust a person they've seen fifty times, not a logo. Won't be that person? Apply anyway. Second place is real.
>
> Three. You fund your own ad spend, from a hundred fifty a day. We run it, you pay the platform.
>
> Four. A team that can take the appointments. If you're the only closer and you're still on the roof, this will break you.
>
> Five. We document the ninety days. Win, and you're the case study.
>
> If that's you, keep watching.

## 1:06 — The hidden lock ✂
> Here's what we found inside the companies we've worked with this year. Lead costs tripled. Your competitors are all running the same ad. And when a lead does come in, it waits.
>
> Six hours. One call. One voicemail. Marked dead. Meanwhile that homeowner filled out three forms and went with whoever picked up first.
>
> That's the whole game now. Not the ad. Not the agency. Recognition and follow-up.

## 1:29 — The master key (show, don't tell)
> Here's what we do inside a business. We structure your offers. We script them. You shoot them. [Cut to three client ads, owner on camera, 5 seconds each.] We edit them, run the advertising, and automate the follow-up so nobody waits six hours.
>
> [Cut to one client's GHL pipeline, blurred names.] Real pipeline. Real appointments. The owner's face is on every ad.
>
> That's the Growth Partner program. Normally three thousand a month, ninety-day minimum. One company gets it free.

## 1:52 — The prizes ✂
> You apply. Thirty seconds. We call within two days and book a twenty-minute interview. Not a sales call.
>
> December fourth we announce the winner: ninety days of Growth Partner, free. Ten thousand dollars of work.
>
> Now second place, and second place is a stack. Free: a one-month Content Package. A forty-five minute interview with me, then ten finished ads and posts in your own words. Twenty-five hundred dollars. A Competitor Analysis: who you're up against, what they're running, and where the gaps are. Fifteen hundred. And five advertising campaigns, scripted for you, ten hooks and a full video script each, ready to shoot. Twelve fifty.
>
> That's five thousand two hundred fifty dollars in work, free, plus half off your first three months of Growth Partner. Fifteen hundred a month instead of three thousand. Nine thousand seven hundred fifty dollars of value, for second place.
>
> And the ROI guarantee: do everything on the list, and if Growth Partner hasn't paid for itself in closed jobs by day ninety, we keep working for free until it has. You've got seventy-two hours to claim it.
>
> One more thing. Refer a company in another market. If they win, you win too.

## 3:12 — Close ✂ (fun again)
> So. Million plus. Face on camera. Ad budget. A team. Willing to be watched.
>
> Hit apply. Thirty seconds. Even second place comes with the stack. If you don't apply, go make a snowman.
>
> Winner's announced December fourth. See you in the interview.

## Ad cuts
- **15s:** Hook lines 1–2 + "Apply. Winner announced December 4."
- **30s:** Hook + Prizes (first and last lines) + Close.
- **60s:** Hook + Before-you-click items 1 and 2 + Prizes + Close.
- **Hook variants to shoot:**
  1. "It's winter. Your door-knockers quit. We're running one roofing company's marketing free for 90 days."
  2. "No AI slop. If you won't put your face in your ads, you won't win this. If you will, it's free."
  3. "Every agency asks if you do a million a year. We're asking if you'll be on camera."
  4. (Santa bit, graphic ad or Higgsfield) Camron in a Santa suit on a roof: "I'm not landing on that one. Nobody's seen an ad for this company since 2019."

## Belief audit
| Brick | Where |
|---|---|
| Outcome | 90 days free; real pipeline on screen |
| Identity | "I'm picking a case study"; the operator who owns their area |
| Problem | Lead costs tripled, same ads, six-hour callback, no recognition |
| Solution | Owner on camera + follow-up automation (the mechanism) |
| Product | Structure, script, shoot, edit, run, automate; $3,000/mo |
| Credibility | Client ads and a real pipeline on screen; the rules themselves |

$1M+ last 12 months · Owner or rep on camera, no AI · Ad spend funded from $150/day · A team that can take appointments · Documented as a case study · Runs through the slow season · $10,000 value · Second-Place Stack: $5,250 in deliverables free + $4,500 off = $9,750 of value (Content Package, Competitor Analysis, 5 scripted campaigns, 50% off 3 months, ROI guarantee)
