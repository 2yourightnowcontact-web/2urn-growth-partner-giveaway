## The idea in one breath
Any AI can make a graphic that says "Low Price", but nobody can copy a roofer's own face and his own sixty-second door pitch, shown to a couple thousand homeowners a day in the zip codes he picks. One roofing company wins ninety days of us building that for free, three win second place, five win third place, and our panel picks all of them on December fifth.

## The script
Spoken numbers are written the way they are said. Each ">" line is one breath. Lines are numbered beat.line (2.3 = beat 2, third spoken line); the ad cuts use these numbers.

### 1. Hook (0:00-0:08)
**SAY**
> David Bradbury sold seventeen roofing projects in nineteen days.
>
> With a face like this.
>
> I'm Camron with 2URN Marketing.

**SHOW** David's own ad, full screen, under 1.1 and 1.2 (this is the "face like this"). Cut to Camron on 1.3. Lower third: "Camron, 2URN Marketing". Under 1.1 and 1.2: "Results from these clients. Yours will differ."

### 2. The flaw (0:08-0:17)
**SAY**
> Your competitors run a graphic that says low price.
>
> Any AI can make that graphic.
>
> A graphic can't earn trust. A face can.

**SHOW** A plain "LOW PRICE" roof graphic (made up, no real company's ad) under 2.1 and 2.2, then back to Camron.

### 3. The door pitch, digital (0:17-0:43)
**SAY**
> You already have the best ad in your market.
>
> Your sixty-second door-knocking pitch.
>
> Today it reaches one door at a time.
>
> Make it fully digital. Selfie style.
>
> A couple thousand people a day, in the zip codes you choose.
>
> Rain, cold, snow or the weekend. Twenty-four seven.
>
> Without a single door knocker touching the ground.
>
> We build your company, not just a lead generation machine. In a way competitors can't copy.

**SHOW** Camron only. Text on 3.5 to 3.7: "A couple thousand homeowners a day" / "Your zip codes" / "Rain, cold, snow, weekends: 24/7".

### 4. The results (0:43-1:12)
**SAY**

>
> In Texas, on as little as a hundred a day.
>

>

**SHOW** Each client's own ad under its lines, 3 to 4 seconds each, with the number as text ("$100,000 in 30 days, Texas", "32X return on ad spend, Knoxville, TN", "70 exclusive leads in 1 month"). Under all of beat 4: "Results from these clients. Yours will differ."

**SHOW** One card per line: "1 grand prize: 90 days of Growth Partner, free. $10,000 of work." / "3 second places: the Second-Place Stack. $5,250 in deliverables, free, plus half off the 90-day program. $9,750 of value." / "5 third places: marketing audit + website. ## Belief audit
- **Outcome:** 1.1 (seventeen roofing projects in nineteen days), all of beat 4, and 7.1 (the next ten-million-dollar roofing company without a people-heavy business).
- **Identity:** 3.1 to 3.3, "You already have the best ad in your market. Your sixty-second door-knocking pitch."
- **Problem:** beat 2, every competitor runs the same "low price" graphic, any AI can make it, and it can't earn trust; 3.3, the pitch reaches one door at a time.
- **Solution:** 3.4 to 3.8, the door pitch made fully digital: his face, a couple thousand people a day, his zip codes, any weather, no door knockers.
- **Product:** beat 5 (1 / 3 / 5 prizes, our panel picks every winner on December fifth) and beat 6 (the terms), with the values on the prize cards.
- **Credibility:** 1.2 with David's ad on screen ("with a face like this"), 1.3 Camron by name, and beat 4, three more named clients with their own ads.

## Do not say
- "Random," "randomly," "drawn," "drawing," "draw" (as picking a winner), "odds," "raffle," "sweepstakes," "hybrid," "instant," "instantly," "on the spot" or "finalist" about any prize or any step. Our panel picks every winner on December fifth, and nobody wins before then.
- "Winners announced," "Winner announced" or "The winner is announced." Say "Winners are picked December fifth."
- "I pick" or "I announce" about any prize. Say "our panel picks" or "we pick."
- "Guaranteed," "guaranteed results," or any promise that the viewer will get these results. The revenue ROI guarantee is on the page, not in this script.
- The counts are public: one grand prize, three second places, five third places. Still never say "everyone wins", "everybody gets", "all applicants" or that missing one place gets you another.
- Any revenue requirement or revenue gate. There is no minimum to enter or to win.
- Any client number other than the four results above, and none of those until its proof is on file.
- Any ad-spend number for second place other than a hundred dollars a day (said "a hundred for second place" in 6.3). The grand prize is said "two hundred a day"; the page carries "$200 to $1,000 a day".
- The Stack's line-by-line prices, or any dollar value on the website. Third place is "no build fee" and "hosting is one eighty-nine a month".
- Anything about how the funnel works behind the scenes, or the 2URN list size.
- Any discount framed as paying, waiving or covering an insurance deductible.
- "Trades," "HVAC," "windows." Roofing only.
