# Setter Script: Interview Calls, Results Calls and Nominee Calls

The call has three jobs:
- **(a) Before the announcement:** book the 20-minute interview for every applicant who did not book it on the booking page, and run it if you are the interviewer. Applicants who booked themselves get no booking call.
- **(b) After the announcement:** deliver the result (grand prize, second place or third place) and book the right call.
- **(c) Call nominated roofers once.** A nominee is a roofer an entrant put forward on the thank-you page. See Part D.

The interview is an interview, not a sales call. Do not pitch on it.

**How each prize is chosen. Say it this way and no other way.**
- **Grand prize:** picked by 2URN's panel on Dec 5 from interviewed applicants, against the published criteria. It is judged. People choose, not chance.
- **Second place:** picked by 2URN's panel on Dec 5 from applicants who completed the interview, on the same published criteria.
- **Third place:** a free audit and a website buildout, picked by 2URN's panel on Dec 5.
- **Never promise anyone a prize.** Nobody wins before Dec 5. Never offer, hint at or hand out the Stack, the half-off price, the audit or the free website before the Dec 5 notification, to anyone, for any reason. They are prizes, and our panel awards them on Dec 5. Never say or hint that they will win. The counts are public: one grand prize, three second places, five third places. Still never say "everyone wins", "everybody gets", "all applicants" or that missing one place gets you another.

**Everyone who enters gets an interview.** Most book it themselves on the booking page right after the form. You call the ones who did not. There is no disqualification on the form. There is **no revenue minimum**. The form asks revenue to prepare the call, and it never disqualifies anyone. Qualification happens here, on the setter call and the interview.

**Not a fit for Growth Partner means one of two things:** they will not be on camera, or they cannot fund $100 a day in ad spend. That is the whole definition. Low revenue alone is never a reason. When a contact is not a fit, they are out of the grand prize and second place, interview reminders stop, and they stay entered for third place. Do not set Do Not Disturb. Do not promise third place.

**Ad budget has three bands.** Under $100 a day: not a fit. $100 to $199 a day: not `not-a-fit`; they stay in for second place and third place and cannot win the grand prize, which needs $200 a day; book, hold and score the interview as normal. $200 a day or more: in for every place. Write the band in the note. Never tell an applicant which place their band puts them in line for. Nobody wins before Dec 5.

**Interview cutoff:** an interview counts for the grand prize and second place only if it is held by the cutoff in the Official Rules (proposed Dec 5, 2026, 5:00 PM CT [CHECK: confirm cutoff with counsel]). An interview held after the cutoff does not count for either. Book every interview before it.

Cadence: your task appears 30 minutes after an entry that did not book, due within 1 hour (entries after 6 PM CT: 9 AM next day). Before the announcement: 3 attempts on day 1, 2 on day 2, 1 on day 3. After the announcement: second-place winners first, then third-place winners who have not booked: 3 attempts on day 1, 2 on day 2, 1 on day 3, all inside the 72-hour claim window. After the window the prize is gone. Any later call (weekly, up to 3 weeks) is about the standard program only and never mentions the prize as available.

Texts: one SMS a day at most per contact, counting workflow texts and setter texts together. Text once, after the last unanswered call of the day, and only if no workflow SMS went to that contact that day. Entrants only, never nominees.

Log outcome in the GHL opportunity stage: Entered / Attempted / Contacted / Interview Booked / Interviewed / Second Place Notified / Second Place Booked / Third Place Notified / Third Place Booked / Nominated / Not a Fit / Not Interested.

Before dialing, read the lead note: company, self-reported revenue, referred-by. The form does not ask about camera, ad budget or team, so ask them on the call. Check the calendar first: if they booked since the task was made, close the task and do not call.

## Part A. Before the announcement: book the interview

### Open (10 seconds)
> Hey {{first}}, it's [Name] with 2URN. You entered our Growth Partner giveaway, and I saw you didn't get to pick a time for your 20-minute interview. I can set it up right now. Got a quick minute?

For a mail-in entry (task "Call to book interview: mail-in"), say instead:
> Hey {{first}}, it's [Name] with 2URN. We got your mail-in entry for our Growth Partner giveaway. I'm calling to set up your 20-minute interview. Got a quick minute?

If busy: "Totally get it. When's better, later today or tomorrow morning?" Book the callback. Don't pitch a busy person.

### Set expectations
> Quick note on how this works. Nobody wins on this call. Our panel picks every winner on December fifth. The interview is how you get judged for the grand prize and second place. It's 20 minutes. It's not a sales call. We ask about your business and see if it fits.

You may say how many prizes there are: one grand prize, three second places, five third places. Do not say anything about what anyone's chances are.

### Book
> I've got [day] at [time] or [day] at [time]. Which one?

Book on the Introduction Interview calendar while on the phone. Use the email and phone already on the contact so the booking attaches to it. Confirm number and email. The calendar emails the time. Send a confirmation text only if no other SMS went to them today.

Book before the interview cutoff (see the header notes). If the only slot the applicant can take is after the cutoff, say this before you book:
> That slot is after the judging cutoff, so the interview won't count for the grand prize or second place. Can we find an earlier time?

If no earlier time works, book it and add "after cutoff" to the opportunity note. The calendar shows no time after Dec 5, 4:40 PM CT. Do not book past it by hand.

**Cancelled or no-show:** workflow D gives you a task "Interview cancelled: rebook" or "No-show: rebook". Same cadence, same open line, but say: "I saw your interview didn't happen. Want to grab a new time?"

## The interview (20 minutes, 2URN interviewer)

**Score each answer and note it** in the opportunity, against the six criteria in the Official Rules. Score the answer as given. Do not coach it up. Every answer goes in the note, including the ones that are a no.

One question per criterion, in this order:

1. **On camera and producing content (criterion 1).** "Who would be on camera in the ads and the content? The owner or a lead rep?" Then: "We don't use AI avatars or stock footage. It has to be a real face homeowners can recognize. Are you comfortable with that, and will you shoot content on your phone?"
2. **Ad budget (criterion 2).** "Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta. The full program runs $200 to $1,000 a day. What could you fund per day?" If the answer is under $200: "Could you fund at least $100 a day?" Note the band: under $100, $100 to $199, or $200 or more.
3. **Case studies for their own business (criterion 3).** "We'll help you turn the results into case studies for your own company, your own jobs and your own numbers. Will you produce those with us?"
4. **Public case study (criterion 4).** "We also document the steps and the process publicly, as a case study on how it was done. Are you good with 2URN telling that story?"
5. **Testimonial for 2URN (criterion 5).** "If it goes well, will you record a testimonial for 2URN Marketing?"
6. **Team capacity and the slow season (criterion 6).** "How many people are on the team? Who answers the phone and who runs the appointments? If we doubled your appointments next month, what happens? And will you run this through the slow season, winter included?"

Then three more questions, context and color, not scored:
- **Revenue, context only.** "What did the company do in revenue over the last 12 months?" This is asked, never required. Any answer is fine. Do not react to the number.
- **The last agency.** "What happened with the last agency or marketer you used?" (Listen for no follow-up, no creative, a budget that never left learning phase. Reflect it back.)
- **Why they want to win.** "Why do you want this, and why now?"

### Not a fit
Only two things make a contact not a fit: they will not be on camera, or they cannot fund $100 a day in ad spend. Revenue never does.
> I appreciate you being straight with me. The full Growth Partner program isn't the right fit right now, based on that. You're still entered, and winners are picked December fifth. Thanks for talking with me.

Then tag `not-a-fit`, set stage Not a Fit, and end the call. Do not promise third place. Do not set Do Not Disturb. The decision is made on the call, not by the form. Tagging stops interview reminders and removes them from the grand prize and second place. They stay entered for third place.

### Close the interview
If they are a fit:
> Thanks, that's what I needed. Our panel picks every winner on December fifth. If you know a roofer in another market that fits, your referral link is still live. If they win the grand prize, you win it too.

## Part B. After the announcement: deliver the result

### Open
> Hey {{first}}, it's [Name] with 2URN. The Growth Partner results are in. Got a minute? Good news.

### Grand prize
Camron calls the grand prize winner within the hour. The setter does not. If the setter reaches them first, say Camron will call within the hour and hand it over. Do not give details.

### Second-place winner
Delivery timing: say when each piece arrives. The Competitor Analysis within 7 days of booking the claim call, whether or not they sign. The Content Package and the five scripted campaigns in month 1 of the engagement. The website, if they need one, is built during the engagement. The Content Package, the campaigns and the website come only with a signed Growth Partner agreement [CHECK: signing deadline]. Do not give a date or a price for the website.

Say it in beats. Stop after each one and let them answer.

> The grand prize went to one company, and our panel picked you for second place. Second place gets you the Second-Place Stack, and it's included free with second place.

> One. A one-month Content Package. A forty-five minute interview with Camron, then we write five ad scripts and five content scripts in your words. You shoot them on your phone with our shot list, and we edit ten finished pieces. That's twenty-five hundred dollars.

> Two. A Competitor Analysis. Who your competitors are right now, what they're running, their offers, hooks, review counts and where the gaps are. Fifteen hundred.

> Three. Five advertising campaigns, scripted for you. Ten hooks and a full video script each, ready to shoot. Twelve fifty.

> Four. If you don't have a website, we build you one.

> That's fifty-two fifty in deliverables plus a website if you need one, free, plus half off your first ninety days of Growth Partner: fifteen hundred a month instead of three thousand, so forty-five hundred off. After that it's three thousand a month. Ninety-seven fifty of value.

> The Competitor Analysis is yours when you book your claim call. The rest is delivered inside the ninety-day program.

> You fund your own ad spend, from a hundred dollars a day, paid straight to Meta.

> It comes with a revenue ROI guarantee. Do these four things: content shot within fourteen days, ad spend funded from two hundred dollars a day, the follow-up sequence live, and the weekly scorecard call. If Growth Partner hasn't paid for itself in closed jobs by day ninety, we keep working for free until it has. The guarantee applies at two hundred dollars a day or more.

> There's a seventy-two-hour clock. The deadline is real. After seventy-two hours the Stack and the half-off price are gone. Claiming just means booking a twenty-minute claim call. On the call we go over what you get, when each piece arrives, and the Growth Partner agreement at fifteen hundred a month for the first ninety days.

The guarantee is its four conditions and nothing else. Do not say "guaranteed results". Do not add to it or soften it. The one sentence that follows it is "The guarantee applies at two hundred dollars a day or more", which restates condition two.

**Book the claim call now:** on the Introduction Interview calendar (the same one as the interview), while on the phone, inside the 72 hours. That booking is the claim, whether or not they already had their interview. It stops the reminders and starts the seven-day Competitor Analysis clock. Confirm number and email, send the confirmation text before hanging up, and update the stage.

**If they say they can't be on camera or can't fund a hundred dollars a day:** do not use the Not a fit script, do not tag `not-a-fit`, and never tell a winner they might not be a fit. Say: "That's what the claim call is for. Booking it gets you the Competitor Analysis either way, and we'll go over the program on the call." Then book the claim call.

A winner who can fund a hundred dollars a day meets second place's minimum. Say: "A hundred a day works for second place. The guarantee applies at two hundred a day or more."

**Not reached inside the 72 hours:** do not tag `not-a-fit`. Log the outcome and stop. Workflow B closes the window and tags `evergreen`. Any later call is about the standard program only and never mentions the prize.

### Anchor (one line)
> A Competitor Analysis free just for booking. Start Growth Partner at half price and the Content Package, the five scripted campaigns and a website if you need one are included.

The longer version, if they ask for the price:
> Standard is three thousand a month, ninety-day minimum. With second place you pay half for the first ninety days. You fund your own ad spend, from a hundred dollars a day, paid straight to Meta. The Competitor Analysis is yours when you book your claim call. The Content Package, the five scripted campaigns and a website if you don't have one are included free inside the program. We script your offers, you shoot them, we edit them, we run your ads, and we automate the follow-up.

## Part D. The nominee call

A nominee is a roofer an entrant put forward on the thank-you page. They have given no consent. They have not entered. Read the call task and the contact note: they name the referrer. The referrer's code is in `first_nominated_by_code` (the first person who nominated them; `nominated_by_code` holds only the latest).

**Rules, no exceptions:**
- **One manual call.** No auto-dialer, no automated text, no automated email.
- **No answer:** leave one voicemail that names the referrer, then stop. No text, no second call.
- **A text only if the nominee asks for the link on the call.** Never before.
- **If they say no:** tag `nominee-declined` and never contact them again.
- A nomination is not an entry. It never changes judging.

### Script
> Hey, it's [Name] with 2URN Marketing. [Referrer first name] at [referrer company] entered our Growth Partner giveaway and put you forward. I'm only calling once. The grand prize is ninety days of Growth Partner free, a ten-thousand-dollar value. The winner funds their own ads, two hundred to a thousand dollars a day, paid to Meta. It takes thirty seconds to enter. Want me to text you the link?

If yes: the nominee is on SMS Do Not Disturb (workflow F). In GHL, turn SMS Do Not Disturb off for this contact, add the note "asked for the link on the call, [date], [setter]", and send the one text. Then turn SMS Do Not Disturb back on. If they enter, workflow A clears it for good.

The text is the **referrer's link**, with the referrer's code from `first_nominated_by_code`, so the referral is matched to the first person who nominated them:

`https://go.2urnmarketing.com/refer?ref=CODE`

If they have questions: "It's for roofing company owners. Every applicant gets a 20-minute interview, win or not. You pick the time right after you enter. The entry page has the rules." Do not promise a prize.

If they say no or ask not to be contacted: "No problem, you won't hear from us again." Tag `nominee-declined`. Never contact them again.

### Voicemail (one only, then stop)
> Hey, it's [Name] with 2URN Marketing. [Referrer first name] at [referrer company] entered our Growth Partner giveaway and put you forward. That's the only reason I'm calling. If you want to look at it, search 2URN Marketing, or call or text this number and I'll send you the link. Either way, I won't call again. Thanks.

### When a referred or nominated roofer enters (optional)
Workflow A notifies Camron. It cannot text the referrer. If you text the referrer, send it by hand from the referrer's own contact, never the nominee's, and only if no other SMS went to the referrer that day:
> 2URN: {{contact.first_name}}, the roofer you referred just entered. If they win the grand prize, you win it too.

## Objections before the announcement (one answer each, then re-ask for the time)
- **"We tried ads, didn't work."** "Yeah, that's what most of the people on this list said. Almost every time we open the account it's no follow-up and a budget that never left learning phase. In the interview we'll look at which one it was for you."
- **"Send me info."** "The page you entered on is the info. The interview is where we look at your business. Tuesday or Thursday?"
- **"Too expensive."** "Could be. That's what the interview is for. If the numbers don't work at your ticket, we'll tell you not to do it."
- **"I don't want to be on camera."** "Understood. For the full program it's required. Homeowners trust a real face, and we don't use AI avatars or stock footage. You're still entered, and winners are picked December fifth. I'd rather tell you now that the full program isn't the right fit." Then tag `not-a-fit` and set stage Not a Fit. Do not promise third place.
- **"I can't do two hundred a day."** "That's fine. The grand prize needs two hundred a day. Second place needs a hundred a day. Could you fund a hundred a day?" If yes, note the band $100 to $199 and re-ask for the time; they are not `not-a-fit`. If no, use the Not a fit script.
- **"We're busy enough."** "Then you're exactly who this is for in the slow season. When does your phone stop ringing, January?" Book for that conversation.
- **"Are you sure I'll win?" / "Do I win something?"** "I can't promise anyone a prize. Our panel picks every winner on December fifth, after the interviews. Nobody wins before then."
- **"Do I need to hit a revenue number?"** "No. There's no revenue minimum. We ask so we can prepare for the call."

## Close the call
> You're booked for [day/time]. You'll get an email with the time and the link. One favor: if you know a roofer in another market that fits, your referral link's still live. If they win the grand prize, you win it too. Talk soon.

## After the call
Update stage. Paste one line in the note: result, who is on camera, ad budget band (under $100, $100 to $199, or $200 or more), case studies and testimonial yes/no, team, next step.
