## 6. Judging criteria
A panel of [3] Sponsor representatives scores each interviewed applicant on the six criteria below, 0 to 100 total. Criteria 1 and 2 are also minimum requirements: an applicant who fails either is not eligible for any prize. Sponsor applies these criteria as published to every judged applicant, and keeps the scorecards. [CHECK: weights are a proposal. Confirm with counsel and Camron before publishing.]

| # | Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eligible company: roofing, HVAC, or window and door company with $1M or more in revenue in the last 12 months (minimum requirement) | 20 |
| 2 | On camera: the owner or a lead rep will appear on camera in the ads. No AI avatars, no stock footage (minimum requirement) | 25 |
| 3 | Ad budget: able to fund advertising spend from $150 per day | 15 |
| 4 | Team capacity: has a team that can handle more appointments, not a one-person operation | 15 |
| 5 | Case study: agrees to be documented, with the 90 days becoming a public case study | 10 |
| 6 | Winter commitment: commits to running the program through the slow season | 15 |

Tie-break: the higher score on criterion 2, then a majority vote of the panel.
