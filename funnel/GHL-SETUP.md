## 2. Inbound webhooks
No GoHighLevel forms are built. GoHighLevel's own form client never sends the flat `forms/submit` body these pages used, so that post failed without any error on the page. The pages post browser JSON to the Inbound Webhook trigger of a workflow instead. Build two workflows with that trigger: A "Giveaway Entry" and F "Nomination" (section 4). Copy each trigger's URL into `live-config.json` (git-ignored; shape in `tools/live-config.example.json`) as `webhook` and `nominate_webhook`, then run `python3 tools/make-live.py`. The repo pages keep `webhook` and `nominateWebhook` as "" (preview mode: the page shows success and sends nothing). Both pages post with `keepalive:true` and `Content-Type: application/json`.

## 4. Workflows
Automation → Workflows.

**A. "Giveaway Entry"**: Trigger: Inbound Webhook. The webhook is the transport (section 2). Copy its URL into `live-config.json` as `webhook`; `01-giveaway-ghl.html` and `04-referral-ghl.html` both post to it. There is no second path into this workflow, so it cannot run twice for one entry. First action, before step 1: Create/Update Contact with the mapping in section 2, then the custom fields, then Add to notes with `{{inboundWebhookRequest.lead_note}}`.
1. **First step, nominees:** if the contact has the tag `nominated`, remove the tag `nominated` and clear Do Not Disturb (SMS and email). They have now entered and consented themselves, so everything below applies to them as normal.
2. **Every entrant:** add tag `giveaway-entrant`. Set `giveaway_result` = entered. There is no form-level Not a Fit branch.
3. Update fields `referral_link` and `interview_link` with the values above. The entrant is on `/interview` picking a time right now, so the steps below that depend on a booking wait for it.
4. Create/Update Opportunity in pipeline "Giveaway" → stage "Entered" (duplicate opportunities off: a nominee who enters already has one at "Nominated"; move that one).
5. Send the email of "On entry (email at once, SMS 10 minutes later)" from `sequence-email-sms.md`.
   5a. Wait 10 minutes (the entrant is on `/interview` picking a time).
   5b. If the contact has the tag `interview-booked`: send "SMS, booked" (section "On entry" in `sequence-email-sms.md`). Else: send "SMS, not booked" (same section). Both are the contact's first SMS and end with "Reply STOP to opt out". Set the SMS step's send window to 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM in the contact's time zone, so a late-night entry gets its text the next morning.
6. Wait until 30 minutes after entry (20 more minutes after 5a). If the contact has the tag `interview-booked`: no setter task.
