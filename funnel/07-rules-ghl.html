2. Eligibility

Open to individuals 18 or older who are the owner or an authorized officer of a roofing company that is licensed (where licensure is required) and operating in the United States at the time of entry. There is no revenue minimum to enter or to win. The entry form asks for a revenue range; the answer is self-reported, is used to prepare for the interview call, and is not an eligibility requirement. Employees of Sponsor, its agencies and immediate family members are not eligible. One entry per business. Void where prohibited. 4. How to enter

Online: complete the entry form at https://2urnagency.com/lead-engine. All required fields must be completed. The form asks for name, company, email, mobile phone and a revenue range. On-camera plan, ad budget and team capacity are confirmed on the call and at interview, not on the form.

Interview: every applicant is offered a 20-minute interview call. The interview is part of judging for the grand prize and the second prize. It is not a sales call and no purchase is discussed as a condition of selection. Applicants who do not complete an interview by the cutoff in Section 3 cannot be selected for the grand prize or the second prize. After submitting the entry form, an applicant may pick an interview time on the booking page. The entry is complete when the form is submitted, whether or not a time is booked. ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM: confirm that showing the booking page between the entry form and the thank-you page does not make booking look like a condition of entry.

Referral: each entrant receives a unique referral link that leads to a referral page. A referral does not add an entry and does not change how an applicant is judged. See Section 5 for the referral bonus. Referred companies must independently meet Section 2 and complete the entry form. Duplicate, fraudulent or ineligible referred entries are void.

Nomination: an entrant may give Sponsor the name and phone number of another roofing company. A nomination is not an entry. The nominated company must complete the entry form itself to enter, and a nomination does not change judging. Sponsor contacts a nominated company by one manual phone call, placed by a person. If there is no answer, Sponsor leaves one voicemail that names the nominating entrant and makes no further contact. Sponsor sends one manual text with the entry link only if the nominated company asks for it on that call, and sends no automated messages (no automated text, no automated email, no auto-dialed call) unless and until the nominated company enters. A nomination earns the nominating entrant no entry and no reward, except the referral bonus in Section 5 if the nominated company enters itself and wins the grand prize. ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM: calling a nominated third party who has given no consent. Confirm this is permitted under the TCPA and state telemarketing and do-not-call rules (including business-to-business and registry rules), what the call script must disclose, how Sponsor handles a "no" (the draft practice is to record it and never contact the company again), whether the nominating entrant must represent that the nominated company agreed to the call, and whether extending the Section 5 referral bonus to a nomination is permitted.

Alternate method of entry (free, no internet): mail a 3x5 card with name, business name, city/state, phone and email to NEEDS: mailing address. One entry per envelope, postmarked by NEEDS: postmark date, received by NEEDS: received-by date. Mail-in applicants are interviewed and judged on the same terms as online applicants. ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM: confirm the mail-in method satisfies any free alternate method of entry requirement.
