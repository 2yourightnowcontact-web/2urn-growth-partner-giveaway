# Valor Office Script — Post-Drawing Calls to Giveaway Entrants

Goal: book the free roof inspection. Not to sell a roof. Every entrant gets called the day after the drawing. Cadence: 3 attempts day 1, 2 on day 2, 1 on day 3, text after each miss, then weekly for 3 weeks. Stage in GHL: Attempted / Contacted / Inspection Booked / Not Interested / Bad Number.

Before dialing, read the note: address, roof age, concern, referred-by.

## Open
> Hi {{first}}, this is [Name] with Valor Roofing in Murfreesboro. You entered our Free Roof Giveaway. I'm calling everybody with the results. Got a quick minute?

Busy: "No problem. Later today or tomorrow morning?" Book the callback.

## Deliver the result
**Second prize:**
> First prize went to one family in [city]. But you're one of the second-prize winners. That's your choice of $2,500 off a full replacement or a free upgrade to Class 4 impact-resistant shingles. There's a 72-hour window to claim it, and the way you claim it is just booking the free inspection. Nothing to buy. We photograph every slope and give you a written report, 45 minutes.

**Not eligible (renter, outside area):** thank them, tag, end.

## Qualify (conversational, 2 questions)
1. "You said the roof is about [age]. Any leaks or missing shingles you've noticed, or is it more that it's getting up there in years?"
2. "Is this the home you plan to be in a while, or are you thinking about selling?" (Selling → a roof certification angle. Staying → longevity and warranty.)

## Anchor (one line, only if they ask about cost)
> A full replacement around here runs in the low to mid teens depending on the size, and with your prize that comes down by $2,500 before anything else. But that's a conversation for after we've seen the roof. The inspection's free either way.

## Book
> Matt or one of our inspectors can be out [day] morning or [day] afternoon. Which is better?
Book on the calendar on the call. Confirm address and phone. Send the confirmation text before hanging up.

## Objections
- **"I'm not buying a roof."** "Good, I'm not selling one today. The inspection is the prize claim. If the roof's fine, you'll have a report that says so, which is handy when you sell."
- **"How much is it going to cost?"** Use the anchor line, then back to booking.
- **"Is this a sales pitch?"** "It's a 45-minute inspection with photos and a written report. Ask Jon Kelley; we fixed his at no charge and left."
- **"My insurance..."** Do not discuss deductibles. "Matt can walk you through what your policy covers when he sees the roof. Let's get him out there."
- **"I'll think about it."** "Totally fine. The claim window is 72 hours though, so let's at least hold a slot. You can move it."

## Close
> You're booked for [day/time]. You'll get a text with the details. One favor: share your link with a neighbor if you haven't. If they win, you get $5,000 off. Talk soon.

## After the call
Update the stage. One line in the note: result, roof age, concern, selling or staying, booked slot.
