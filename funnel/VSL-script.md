# 2URN Growth Partner Giveaway: VSL Script (v7, Nov 24: the full VSL)

**Runtime:** about 9:26 at 150 words a minute. 1,414 spoken words (the ">" lines only).
**On camera:** Camron, alone, on his phone. Selfie style, two locations: location one is a desk or office (beats 1, 2, 5 to 10, 12 to 15), location two is the truck cab or the driveway with a coat on (beats 3, 4, 11, and the one Tuesday-in-January shot in 8). Phone on a tripod or a steady hand at eye height, an arm's length away, a window or soft light in front of him, never behind. Eye line on the lens, not the screen. One take per beat, fifteen takes, each one under a minute and a half except beat 9 (about 1:34), which is shot as two takes split before 9.10 (the Second-Place Stack); a teleprompter app on the same phone is fine, scrolled at about 150 words a minute. Shoot beat 1 last, once the voice is warm. Plan on about two hours for the whole shoot, including resets, a coat change and a second take of any beat that stumbles. Shoot date: [DECIDE: reshoot date]
**Edit:** Shabi. Jump cuts on the breath, captions burned in, a one-second chapter card at the top of every beat after the hook, client ads and simple text cards where SHOW says so. The picture changes at least every ten seconds: Camron, a card, a client ad, back to Camron. Nothing a phone editor can't make.
**Where it runs:** the video at the top of `/lead-engine` (01) and `/refer` (04). The two-minute cut below runs where nine minutes won't fit, plus the 15, 30 and 60-second ad cuts.

## The idea in one breath
A roofing owner spends every winter chasing the next job: door knockers to manage, leads shared with four other roofers, and a phone nobody answers in five minutes. In ninety days we turn his own sixty-second door pitch into ads with his face on them, run in his ad account, with his own people calling every lead in five minutes, and hand him an engine his team runs, a name his town sees every day, and his week back. It's three thousand a month; one roofing company gets it free, three get the Second-Place Stack, five get third place, and our panel picks all of them on December fifth.
